Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Milad media uploader is a private tool used only by Milad Fahmy to upload videos he made to his own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

It uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload permission, only on Milad Fahmy's own account, to upload videos and set their titles and descriptions.

What it stores and shares

It does not collect, store, sell or share data about anyone else. The access token is kept in the owner's private settings and used only to upload his videos.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
